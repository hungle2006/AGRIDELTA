# AGRIDELTA automatic Blender care revision

Requested correction: automatic visible water and fertilizer in the Blender scene, red tomato fruit, a connected root canopy on desktop and phones, calmer guide arrangement and a dimensional app mark. No manual care buttons.

Implementation: watering can + 15 moving drops + 10 animated nutrient pellets, synchronized with the existing 28-second growth clip. Three red tomato meshes have green calyxes. Five root systems now share the exact stem collar; lateral and absorbing branches are sampled from their parent centrelines. Mobile spreads roots to the available width and keeps topic nodes at their tips rather than in a detached rail. Detailed instructions sit in one panel after the scene. The original mark is rendered as an extruded Blender relief.

Validation in progress: automated lifecycle, guide, asset and export checks; Vite build; browser preview at 320/390/768/1440 and reference dimensions.

Environment: browser WebGL is disabled, so browser checks exercise the moving Blender fallback. Direct GLB animation and anatomy are validated from source and exported assets; no physical iPhone or direct GPU visual verification is claimed.
